Delivery FAQ

What is the standard delivery radius? expand_more
Our standard local delivery covers a 15-mile radius from our main kitchen. If you are outside this zone, please contact us for custom delivery options.
Is there a minimum order for delivery? expand_more
Yes, standard local deliveries require a minimum order of $35. Catering and scheduled deliveries have different minimums depending on the package.
How do I track my order? expand_more
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email notification with a live tracking link to follow your delivery driver in real-time.
